nimbi app #3 · a focus timer · options · 12 Oct 2026

A little cloud that keeps time while you work

A Pomodoro timer in the menu bar. The cloud does the job: it changes shape between focus and rest, shows its mood when time is up, and plays the sounds you pick. Below are the decisions to make before any view code is written. Each one has a recommended option.

The phase bar drives every live cloud on this page. A whole 25-minute focus session is squeezed into about ten seconds so you can watch the morphs. Move the pointer over a stage and the eyes follow it.

Decision 1

The name

One plain, capitalized word, with "by nimbi" after it (DESIGN.md 1). Each of these ties time to the sky, so it sits next to Relay and Skyline without saying "pomodoro" or "timer".

Tempo by nimbiRecommended
Tempo
Italian for both "time" and "weather". Pomodoro is Italian too.
"Tempo: the weather of your work."

Also means pace in music, which suits work and rest in turns. Short and easy to say. The one catch: the word is common, so there are other apps called Tempo, and a store search will be crowded.

Spell by nimbi
Spell
"A sunny spell", "a spell of work", and a bit of magic.
"One spell of focus. Then a cloud break."

Fits the cosmic, slightly mystic side of the brand. Could be read as a spell checker at first glance.

Lull by nimbi
Lull
The calm between two storms. Here, the rest that earns the next push.
"Work hard. Lull often."

Gentle and memorable. It names the break more than the focus.

Squall by nimbi
Squall
A short, sudden burst of weather. Here, 25 minutes of focus.
"Short storms of focus."

The cheekiest of the four. Sounds loud for an app that wants you calm.

Decision 2

How the cloud morphs

You asked for morph animations, so here the cloud's outline does change. Skin, face, eyes, gaze and moods stay as they are in every nimbi app. DESIGN.md 3.5 says the outline stays unchanged, so whichever you pick gets one new line there: "this app's cloud may change shape with the timer; skin, face, eyes and moods stay."

A · Condense and puff

Recommended
Focus25:00
  • Always the cloud. Focus pulls its scallops in to a smooth, dense orb; a break puffs it out wide; a long break lays it down flat. Time's up, it bursts into a star-like puff.
  • Every morph is a springy squash and stretch, so it reads well at 40 pt in the panel and 24 pt at the notch.
  • Subtle at a glance: you notice the shape, not the minutes. The ring and the digits tell the time.

B · The cloud becomes the tool

Focus25:00
  • The most morph you can get: an hourglass with draining sand, a ringing bell, a steaming mug, a sleepy crescent moon. Each one says the phase at once.
  • Fun to watch, and great on the website.
  • Most of the time it isn't a cloud, which works against "every app has the same cloud". Thin shapes (the hourglass waist) get muddy below 30 pt.

C · Rain it out

Focus25:00
  • The cloud is the timer: it rains your focus into a pool, shrinking as the minutes go. On a break the pool steams back up and the cloud grows again.
  • The most weather-like idea, and time is readable from the mascot alone.
  • The shape change is a slow shrink, not a snappy morph, so it's calmer than A or B. A small cloud at minute 24 could look sad.

Decision 4

The menu bar item

A template glyph, so macOS tints it (DESIGN.md 11.3). Color only when the timer needs you: an amber dot when time is up and nothing has started the next phase.

A · Cloud and minutes

Recommended
  • The minutes are always readable, like Skyline's temperature. Only whole minutes (18′) until the last one, so it doesn't twitch every second.
  • Takes the most room in a crowded menu bar.

B · A cloud that drains

  • The glyph itself is the timer: solid at the start, emptying from the top as the minutes go. Narrowest.
  • You can tell "about half", not "18 minutes". Hard to read at 16 pt.

C · Minutes only while running

  • At rest, just the cloud. Running, just the minutes with a small dot for the phase.
  • The cloud disappears exactly when it's doing its job, and the item's width jumps.
Decision 5

Sounds

You asked for customizable sounds. DESIGN.md 10 allows only macOS system sounds and never loops, so B and C each need a line there. Every sound has its own picker, a volume, a preview, and one switch turns them all off. The previews below are synthesized in your browser, the same way the app would make them; nothing is downloaded.

A · System sounds per event

Focus startsPurrFocus endsGlassBreak endsTinkDaily goalPop
  • Exactly what DESIGN.md asks for. Any of the eight system sounds for each event.
  • Familiar, and so a little anonymous. Nothing to listen to while you work.

B · A, plus weather while you focus

Recommended
  • A cloud that rains while you work. Off by default, picked by you, fades in when focus starts and out at the break. Made in the app from noise, so no audio files and no licenses.
  • Fits option C of the cloud perfectly, and the others well.
  • A loop, which DESIGN.md forbids for notifications. It needs the line "ambience the user turns on is not a notification".

C · B, plus nimbi's own chimes

  • Soft glassy tones that sound like nimbi, offered next to the system sounds in every picker.
  • A new kind of asset for the family. Relay and Skyline would want them too, so they'd belong in NimbiKit and DESIGN.md.
Decision 6

The app icon's prop

The standard tile and cloud (DESIGN.md 11.2) with one prop in aurora or one state color. Shown at 128 and 32 px.

A · A ring of time

Recommended
  • An aurora arc three quarters round the cloud: a timer, and the same ring as the panel.
  • The arc must stay thick to survive at 16 px.

B · A small hourglass

  • Says "time" to everyone.
  • A second object next to the cloud; generic.

C · Dial ticks

  • Twelve ticks round the cloud, like a kitchen timer's face.
  • Turns to grey mush at 32 px and below.
