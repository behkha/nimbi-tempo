Tempo at the notch

Tempo's page for the shared notch island, in DESIGN.md 18.4's page format. Closed, the island shows the cloud in its current tool and a short glance. Hover it and it opens into the page. As with Skyline, Tempo takes the notch only when you pick it in settings ("Use for Tempo"), and hands it back with one click.

Decision 1

The open island

580 wide, the notch plus 160 tall. The cloud on the left (92 pt), Tempo's name, a title, a subtitle, and up to two actions. The first action is white only when time is up (18.4). On the right sits the detail block.

A · A ring on the right

Recommended
  • Exactly 18.4's ring detail block, so the page carries over unchanged to the shared island in phase 2.
  • The ring matches the panel's, so the island reads as a small copy of it.
  • The cloud and the ring sit apart, unlike the panel where the cloud is inside its ring.

B · The cloud in its ring

  • Most like the panel: the cloud in a ring, big digits for the title.
  • A ring round the page's cloud isn't part of the page format. The shared island in phase 2 couldn't draw it, so the page would change then.

C · Rows

  • Calmest. The hourglass's sand shows the progress, and the rows add the cycle and today.
  • No glanceable progress shape apart from the sand, which is small at 92 pt.
Decision 2

The closed island

The notch plus two 60 pt wings, so it hides as few menu bar items as possible. The left wing holds the cloud at 24 pt in its current tool; the right wing holds the glance (at most 14 characters). An amber dot means time is up and the next phase waits.

A · Cloud and minutes

Recommended
  • Same as the menu bar item, so it reads the same wherever Tempo lives.
  • You can still see the tool: hourglass, bell, mug, moon.
  • The hourglass and bell are thin at 24 pt.

B · Cloud in a ring, and minutes

  • Progress at a glance, even without reading the number.
  • The ring squeezes the cloud down to about 18 pt inside a 32 pt wing.

C · Cloud and a little ring

  • Quietest. Nothing ticks while you work.
  • No number. You have to hover to see how long is left.

The plan, either way

  • Ownership (DESIGN.md 18.2): Tempo never takes the notch on its own. Settings name the owner ("Notch is used by Relay") with Use for Tempo, and Give it to Relay back. While Tempo owns it, the menu bar item steps aside.
  • Actions: while running, Pause space and Timer ↩. When time is up, Start break ↩ is white (needsYou) next to Skip S. Timer opens Tempo's panel hanging flush from the island.
  • Motion: the island opens after 0.2 s of hover and closes 0.6 s after the pointer leaves. The cloud's morph carries on inside it.
  • Shared code: Skyline's island window, hover and hang logic move into NimbiKit, so Tempo uses them instead of copying. Switching Skyline over to the shared version comes later.
