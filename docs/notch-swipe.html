Swipe the notch from app to app

Today the notch belongs to one nimbi app at a time, and you move it with a button in each app's settings. With this change, the open island gets a page per running app (Relay, Skyline, Tempo). Swipe sideways with two fingers, drag it, or press ← → to move between them. The motion is Drift from DESIGN.md 18.8: pages move like clouds at different heights.

Try it

Two-finger swipe, drag, or click the island and press ← →. Click a dot to jump.

Decided

Every app keeps its own notch

A swipe hands the notch to the next app, which opens its own island right away with its content drifting in from the side. Relay's notch stays exactly as it is. Skyline and Tempo notice a two-finger swipe over Relay's island and hand the notch on; Relay already steps aside whenever another app takes the notch. Relay's island has no dots, because its code doesn't change.

Decision

Which apps get a page

Running apps only

Recommended
  • Every page is live and the swipe is instant.
  • A quit app drops out of the dots until you open it again.

Installed apps, launched on arrival

  • The dots always show the whole family you have.
  • Swiping to an app that isn't running waits a second or two while it launches, and it starts apps you'd closed.

The plan, either way

  • Gestures (DESIGN.md 18.6): a two-finger swipe moves one page once it passes 46 pt, then locks until the gesture has been quiet for 220 ms. Dragging with the mouse follows the pointer with 30% resistance at the ends, and commits past 18% of the width or 0.45 pt/ms. Click the island, then ← → to step through pages. ⌃⌥← and ⌃⌥→ work from anywhere. Click a dot to jump, and esc closes.
  • Dots (18.7): 12 pt from the bottom of the open island. The current page is a 16 × 6 white capsule and the others are 6 pt dots. An app that needs you on another page glows amber and pulses. The closed island shows 4 pt mini dots after the glance.
  • Urgency (18.5): a page that needs you comes to the front only while the island is closed. A swipe beats data: after you swipe away, a page comes back by itself only if its urgency rises.
  • Relay, unchanged: it's in the swipe while it's running and set to live at the notch. A swipe over its island is picked up by one of the other running nimbi apps, which hands the notch on. ⌃⌥← and ⌃⌥→ work while Relay has the notch too.
  • Not in this round: a page that needs you jumping to the front by itself. That would move the notch without you asking (18.2). An amber dot on the closed island says so instead.
  • The buttons in settings stay, for picking the app that's at the notch when you start up.
